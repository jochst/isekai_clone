# Transparent Pricing
            Hi guys, I'm Louis, founder of ISEKAI ZERO!
Let me break down how our pricing works and where your money actually goes.
### Why This Page Isn't Available in Our Mobile Apps
Apple and Google take a 30-34% tax (depending on region) on in-app purchases, so they prohibit us from displaying this pricing information in their apps if we offer alternative payment methods outside their stores.
### What We're Building Here
We imagine a platform that is truly community-focused, built from the ground up to give you the ultimate AI roleplay experience. This is why we made our platform token usage-based with transparent pricing. We don't enforce hidden memory limits, we don't enforce private models—by default, we use DeepSeek v4 Flash 0731, which is extremely affordable and delivers consistent, coherent RP. But of course, if you want an even better experience, you can use our premium selection.
We believe you deserve to know exactly what you're paying for and where every cent goes. No hidden fees, no mysterious calculations—just honest, straightforward pricing.
##### The Exchange Rate
100 Arcane = $1 USD (base rate)
Payment processing fees are added on top depending on your payment method (Website: ~10%, App Stores: ~34%).
### Why We Chose Token-Based Pricing
The beauty of our token-based platform is complete freedom over your preferences. Subscription services are forced to limit what they can offer based on the constraints of their monthly budget, but with us, you choose exactly what you want.
Of course, there are trade-offs—here you pay per use, while subscriptions work within a fixed monthly budget. However, you'd be surprised how great the roleplay experience is and how far you can go with just $1.
##### What You Get for Your Money:
$1 (100 Arcane) on DeepSeek v4 Flash 0731: Up to 1,000 full memory RP messages
With our chat summarization feature (up to 80% context saved!), your credits stretch even further.

## FAQ
Q: Why is pricing different on mobile apps?
A: Apple and Google take 30-34% platform fees, which are passed to users. We legally can't display this pricing information in-app. That's why we keep recommending the website where fees are only ~10%.
Q: Do my credits expire?
A: Nope! Your Arcane credits never expire. We're not going to punish you for not using the platform constantly.
Q: Can I see exactly how much each message costs?
A: Absolutely! Tap on any message and click the info button—a full breakdown will be provided showing the exact Arcane cost and where it goes.
Q: What if I run out of credits?
A: You can always switch to our free models, watch ads to earn extra Mana, or purchase more credits on our website.
Q: How do creators get paid?
A: Creators automatically receive 25% of the profit from any paid credits used with their storylines. It's tracked and distributed automatically—no manual work needed. Payments are processed daily at 12am GMT+8.
